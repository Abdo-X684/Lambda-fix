Lambda-fix 1.1.2 - Windows package
==================================

PushThink IS DELETED
--------------------
You asked why I could not just delete it. I could, and I should have. It is
gone, along with FindTouchingEntity. The DLL no longer contains either name.

WHY IT WAS THERE, AND WHY THAT WAS WRONG
----------------------------------------
1.0.4 shipped the upstream bhl-op4 CPushable::Move, which carries the HL25
fix. That version applies an impulse scaled by frametime while the engine
applies player friction once per frame, so above ~60 fps the box's velocity
decays to nothing. The box did not move, and I concluded that HL25 was not
delivering Touch() to a MOVETYPE_PUSHSTEP entity at all.

That conclusion was wrong, and your binaries prove it:

    1.0.4 DLL          PushThink ABSENT   box does not move
    your server DLL    PushThink ABSENT   box moves fine
    our 1.0.9 DLL      PushThink PRESENT  box moves

Your working server never needed a think. It moves boxes through Touch() and
Use() with the pre-HL25 Move(). I copied that Move() but kept the think, so
every build from 1.0.9 on had one piece of code in the physics path that your
server does not have - and that is where the boost went wrong.

WHAT THIS BUILD IS
------------------
func_break.cpp is now the working server's CPushable, with nothing added:

    Move()   verbatim, pre-HL25 form (no fudge, IN_FORWARD|IN_USE on push,
             clamp only on push, accumulate + player/box velocity sync)
    Use()    verbatim  -> Move(player, 0)   <- the boost, unclamped
    Touch()  verbatim  -> Move(player, 1)
    Spawn()  verbatim - no SetThink, no nextthink
    no PushThink, no FindTouchingEntity, no overlap scan, no per-frame code

Verified in the compiled DLL:
    ?PushThink@CPushable@@QAEXXZ          absent
    FindTouchingEntity                    absent
    func_pushable                         registered (2 strings)
    Move() push gate                      test byte ptr [ebx+0x180], 0x28
                                          (IN_FORWARD|IN_USE) - pre-HL25 form
    items/flashlight1.wav + flashlight2.wav   both present (on/off fixed)

NIGHT VISION SOUND
------------------
Unchanged from 1.1.0: SOUND_FLASHLIGHT_OFF is items/flashlight2.wav, matching
OP4 dlls/player.h:64. Upstream Valve's dlls/player.h:61 has flashlight1 for
both, which is what bhl-op4 inherited.

WHAT I AM NOT CLAIMING
----------------------
I have not run this in game. Deleting the think assumes Touch() really is
delivered in this build, which is what your server's behaviour implies but
which I cannot execute here. If the box turns out not to move at all, that is
the answer: Touch is not delivered, and the think was masking it. The banner
plus the fact that the box either moves or does not will settle it in one
test.
